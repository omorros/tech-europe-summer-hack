Scope: the three demo routes — `/` address entry, `/phone` mock caller, `/console` dispatch. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the dispatch operator reads an incident picture assembling itself while a 999 call runs; they never configure. The caller has one button. The judging panel watches one projected screen for five minutes and must follow the story without narration.

Direction: **The Incident Log** (assigned by the roll, seed 14f187c3, candidate 5 of 7; challengers Seven-Segment Panel and Timetable Slide Rack were competitive and declined). The console is the incident record writing itself, not a dashboard watching one. A true-white printed roll on near-black steel; sprocket margin and timestamps; one monospace record size and one huge condensed size with nothing in between; square corners and reversed stamp bars; deep voids instead of card chrome.

Memorable moment: transcript fragments print in carbon grey and strike to solid ink when final, with extracted entities stamped in red into the line where they were said — not filed to a sidebar.

Colour law: red reports incident state, hi-vis yellow reports the operator's own actions, nothing else is coloured. If a colour is not reporting a fact, it comes out.

Two phases: panel console during the call; on `briefing.ready` one authored transformation gathers every panel down into the bottom-left corner while the video takes the screen, and the corner menu reopens them as windows over it.

Unresolved: which video owns the full-bleed frame (fal reconstruction or the VEED brief) — treated as one placeholder slot because the user is not building the backend this pass.
